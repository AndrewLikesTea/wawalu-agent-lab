The display-name notice on Social, People and the post page now separates two cases: posts that were already on Social, and posts somebody published. It no longer calls a feed of real, permanent posts a "demo" (#2348). This PR changes only that notice's wording.

## The sentence

Old, identical on all three pages:

> Display names are invented for this demo or chosen by whoever published the post — nobody owns or verifies one, and anyone can publish under any name.

New, identical on all three pages:

> Display names on the posts already on Social are invented. On any other post, whoever published it chose the name. Nobody owns or verifies a display name, and anyone can publish under any name.

- No "demo".
- "Invented" is the only word it uses for made-up content. It matches each page's own intro: Social says "The posts already here are invented…", People says "The image posts already here are invented…", and the post page says "The posts already on Social are invented…".
- It keeps "nobody owns or verifies" and "anyone can publish under any name".
- It doesn't say "published as", and it implies nothing about posts existing only in this browser.
