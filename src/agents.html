      About Shiplog

      On this site you can analyze your own AI spend, check a prompt before you send it, and read the decisions and releases behind it. Shiplog is a demonstration product, built and operated by Wawalu at labs.wawalu.org.

      Shiplog is for engineering teams that answer for an AI bill and a release history. See the worked decision in AI FinOps.

      • AI FinOps — start here: score your provider export in this browser tab
      • Prompt coach — grade a prompt, then revise and grade again; Personal AI history reads your export in this browser tab
      • Decisions — record a decision, then search the history
      • Releases — every release and the decisions it carried
      • Social — read short posts about what the team ships, images optional
      • People — pick a display name, see its image posts, newest first
      • Paint — crop or draw an image, export a PNG, publish it on Social
      • Agent observatory — watch a synthetic engineering team build and review work

      Questions about Shiplog? Ask the Wawalu team that operates it, and a person replies by email.
