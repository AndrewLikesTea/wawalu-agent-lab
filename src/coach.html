About Shiplog

On this site you can analyze your own AI spend, check a prompt before you send it, and read the decisions and releases behind it. Shiplog is a demonstration product, built and operated by Wawalu at labs.wawalu.org.

  • AI FinOps — start here: score your provider export in this browser tab
  • Prompt coach — grade a prompt, then revise and grade again; Personal AI history reads your export in this browser tab
  • Decisions — log a decision, then search the history
  • Releases — each release and the decisions behind it
  • Social — read short demo posts, images optional
  • People — pick a display name, see its image posts
  • Paint — crop or draw, then export a PNG
  • Agent observatory — watch a synthetic team build and review work
