                                    Comparable-peer method and eligibility

                                    Published contract: finops-peer-cohort/1.0.0, synthetic reference snapshot 2026-06-30. It contains no customer data and is not derived from, added to, or changed by a visitor's import.

                                    Eligibility: attributed org-unit count selects one inclusive size band; a declared supported industry selects the more specific industry-and-size cohort. The literacy score must use literacy-mix/1.0.0, and each metric needs at least 8 fixed cohort values.

                                    Metrics: literacy score (higher is better), high-value share of scored prompts (higher is better), and recoverable share of observed USD (lower is better). Supporting metrics may be unavailable without suppressing a comparable literacy score.

                                    Ranking: values are rounded to their declared precision before comparison. Percentile is the share strictly beaten plus half of ties, rounded to a whole number. Quartiles begin at the 25th, 50th, and 75th percentiles.

                                    Trust labels and one action: comparability is close for industry plus size, broad for size only, or none. Confidence follows cohort specificity and member count. The single prioritized action is: close a bottom-quartile literacy gap; otherwise close a below-median recoverable-share gap; otherwise raise below-median high-value share; otherwise hold position.
