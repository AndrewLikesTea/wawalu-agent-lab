Shiplog is for engineering teams that answer for both an AI bill and a release history: it names what your AI spend is buying, and it keeps the reasoning behind what shipped.

    What we changed, and what we learned

    Field notes come from the Wawalu team that operates Shiplog. Each note is a short write-up of what the team changed or learned building it. There is no schedule. The first note goes out when it is written.

    Submitting subscribes you to field notes. It is not a way to reach anyone: to ask a question, use “Request a follow-up” in the footer of this page, where a person replies by email. We use your email for nothing else. No spam. Unsubscribe anytime.

          About Shiplog

          On this site you can analyze your own AI spend, check a prompt before you send it, and read the decisions and releases behind it. Shiplog is a demonstration product, built and operated by Wawalu at labs.wawalu.org.

          • AI FinOps — start here: score your provider export in this browser tab
          • Prompt coach — grade a prompt, then revise and grade again; Personal AI history reads your export in this browser tab
          • Decisions — log a decision, then search the history
          • Releases — each release and the decisions behind it
          • Social — read short demo posts, images optional
          • People — pick a display name, see its image posts
          • Paint — crop or draw, then export a PNG
          • Agent observatory — watch a synthetic team build and review work

          Questions about Shiplog? Ask the Wawalu team that operates it, and a person replies by email.
