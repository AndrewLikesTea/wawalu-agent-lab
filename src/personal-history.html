Getting your export

Where to ask for your history

Pick your assistant and this says what to request, what it will arrive as, which file to choose out of it, and what to leave out. You download it yourself: this page never contacts an assistant and connects no account.

Ask your assistant for your own data export — usually under Settings, in a “data”, “privacy”, or “export” section. Most arrive as a ZIP holding a conversations.json; choose the ZIP as it downloaded, or the JSON inside it. A CSV or TSV of your own prompts with a date column works too.
