To ask the Wawalu team to review a post, select Report post on it.

How reporting works: Report post opens a short form about that one post. Choose a reason, add a note if you want to, and give your email address. The report goes only to the Wawalu team, who review each one. A report does not remove or hide the post, and not every report leads to removal.

About Shiplog

On this site you can analyze your own AI spend, check a prompt before you send it, and read the decisions and releases behind it. Shiplog is a demonstration product, built and operated by Wawalu at labs.wawalu.org.

Shiplog is for engineering teams that answer for an AI bill and a release history. See the worked decision in AI FinOps.
