Report post

A report goes to the Wawalu team for review. It does not delete the post, and not every report leads to removal.

Choose “Report post” on a post in Social or People.

We send the selected post’s ID, your reason, optional context, follow-up email, and a random submission ID to Wawalu. We also store the receipt time. Your report and email are stored privately for team review, never in public posts or People listings. No image bytes or other posts are included. Standard connection metadata is received by the hosting service.
