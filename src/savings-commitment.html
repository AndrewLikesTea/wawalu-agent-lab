About Shiplog

On this site you can analyze your own AI spend, check a prompt before you send it, and read the decisions and releases behind it. Shiplog is a demonstration product, built and operated by Wawalu at labs.wawalu.org.

  • AI FinOps — Find where to act first on your AI spend: score your own provider export in this browser tab. Start here.
  • Prompt coach — Grade one prompt against a bundled rubric in your browser tab, then revise it and grade again.
  • Decisions — Record a decision with its context, alternatives, and owner, then search and filter the history.
  • Releases — Every release, newest first, with the decisions it carried.
  • Social — Social is a shared demo feed of short posts about the work the team ships, each with an optional image.
  • People — Pick a demo persona and see that person's image posts, newest first. The picker is on the page.
  • Paint — Draw or crop an image in this tab, export a PNG, then hand it to a Social post yourself.
  • Agent observatory — Watch a synthetic engineering team plan, build, review, and deliver work.
