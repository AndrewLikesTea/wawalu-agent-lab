Shiplog evaluation brief

Engineering teams need to explain why work shipped without reconstructing decisions from chat history. Shiplog records the context, alternatives, and owner of a decision and links it to the release it shaped, so teammates can trace shipped work back to its reasoning.

Decisions and releases you add stay in this browser; they are not shared hosted records. Shiplog is a demonstration product built and operated by Wawalu. Availability and pricing are provided on request.

The example decisions and releases are invented, use no customer or production data, and are not customer results. To verify what this site shipped, open the real record of this deployment.

Public deployment evidence: https://labs.wawalu.org/releases.html#shipped-build
