      About Shiplog

      Shiplog is a demonstration engineering decision and release log, built and operated by Wawalu at labs.wawalu.org.

      Questions about Shiplog? Ask the Wawalu team that operates it, and a person replies by email.

      Submitting sends a Shiplog follow-up request. This form sends one thing: the work email address you type. Nothing else on this page — nothing you have read, filtered, imported, or exported — is read, attached, or transmitted, and the address goes to the Wawalu team that operates Shiplog.
